Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about TIPFIN loans — application, approval, documents, fees, and payout.

📝 Application
How do I apply for a loan?+

Visit our online application page at apply.html and fill in the form. You will need to choose your loan type, amount, term, and upload the required documents. The entire process takes approximately 5–10 minutes.

What documents do I need to apply?+

Required documents depend on your loan type, but generally include:

  • Valid South African ID or passport
  • Latest 3 months' payslips or proof of income
  • Latest 3 months' bank statements
  • Proof of residence (not older than 3 months)
  • Proof of business registration (for business loans)
Can I apply on my mobile phone?+

Yes! Our platform is fully mobile-optimised. You can complete your entire application, upload documents and track your status directly from your smartphone. Our TIPFIN app is also available for download on Android and iOS.

Is there a fee to apply?+

No. Submitting an application through TIPFIN is completely free of charge. You will never be asked to pay anything before receiving your approved funds.

Can I apply in a different currency?+

TIPFIN supports multiple currencies including ZAR, USD, EUR, GBP, NGN, KES, GHS, TZS, UGX, ZMW, MWK, RWF, BIF, SSP, MZN, AOA, CDF, MGA, KMF, DJF, XAF, XOF, MVR and SZL. Select your preferred currency during the application process.

⚡ Approval
How long does approval take?+

Most first-time applicants receive an approval decision within 30 minutes during business hours. If you have applied within the past 30 days and provided valid documents, your application may be pre-approved even faster.

What happens if my application is declined?+

If your application is declined, we will notify you with a reason. You are welcome to reapply after 30 days or contact us via WhatsApp for guidance on improving your application. Common reasons for decline include insufficient income documentation or incomplete uploads.

Will a credit check affect my score?+

Our initial application review is a soft inquiry and does not affect your credit score. A formal credit check may be required for final loan approval, which may appear as an inquiry on your credit record.

What if I was approved before — do I need to reapply?+

If you have been approved within the last 30 days, you do not need to reapply. Contact us on WhatsApp at +27 786 216 617 and we can assist with completing your payout directly.

📂 Documents
What file formats can I upload?+

We accept PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, and HEIC file formats. Files must be clearly legible and no larger than 10MB each. You can take photos of documents directly with your smartphone.

Are my uploaded documents secure?+

Yes. All uploaded documents are encrypted using 256-bit SSL during transfer and stored in a secure, POPIA-compliant cloud environment. Only authorised TIPFIN staff can access your documents for the purpose of processing your application.

Can I re-upload a document after submission?+

Yes. If a document needs to be replaced or updated, please contact us via WhatsApp at +27 786 216 617 and our team will assist you in uploading the replacement document.

💸 Payout
My loan was approved — when do I receive the funds?+

Loan funds are not disbursed automatically. Once your application is approved, you must contact TIPFIN (PTY) LTD directly via WhatsApp at +27 786 216 617 or call us at +27 786 216 617 to complete the final payout verification. Funds are typically disbursed within 24 hours of payout completion.

Which bank accounts can receive the payout?+

We can disburse funds to any valid South African bank account including FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, Capitec, and all major banks. The account must be in your name.

Can someone else receive the funds on my behalf?+

No. For security and compliance purposes, all loan disbursements must be made to the bank account of the primary applicant. Third-party disbursements are not permitted.

💰 Fees & Rates
What interest rate does TIPFIN charge?+

TIPFIN charges a fixed 5% interest rate on all loan products. For example, a loan of R10,000 over 12 months will have a total repayment of R10,500 (R875/month). There are no variable rates, no hidden fees, and no surprises.

Are there any other fees or charges?+

No. TIPFIN does not charge application fees, admin fees, initiation fees, or monthly service fees beyond the stated 5% interest. All costs are disclosed upfront before you accept any loan offer.

Is there an early settlement penalty?+

No. You may settle your loan early at any time without penalty. Simply contact us to get an early settlement quote. Interest is calculated only on the outstanding balance.

✅ Eligibility
Who can apply for a TIPFIN loan?+

To qualify for a TIPFIN loan you must be:

  • 18 years of age or older
  • Able to demonstrate ability to repay (income or business revenue)
  • In possession of a valid government-issued ID or passport
  • The holder of an active bank account for fund disbursement
Can self-employed applicants apply?+

Yes. Self-employed individuals and business owners are welcome to apply. You will need to provide recent bank statements, proof of business registration or trading evidence, and confirmation of income.

Do I need a good credit score?+

We evaluate all applications on a case-by-case basis. While a positive credit history helps, we also consider other factors including income stability, existing debt obligations and business performance. We encourage all applicants to apply and see.

🔐 Privacy & Security
How does TIPFIN protect my personal information?+

All personal data is protected under our POPIA-compliant privacy policy. We use 256-bit SSL encryption for all data transfers and store documents in a secured cloud environment with strict access controls. We never sell or share your data with third parties without your consent.

Can I request deletion of my data?+

Yes. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at info.tipfin@aol.com. Please note that certain records may be retained for legal and regulatory compliance purposes in line with POPIA regulations.

Is TIPFIN a registered financial institution?+

Yes. TIPFIN (PTY) LTD is a registered credit provider with the National Credit Regulator (NCR) under registration number NCRCP22024. Our legal company registration number is 2024/669652/07. We comply fully with the National Credit Act and POPIA.
